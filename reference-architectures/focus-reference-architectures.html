Build or buy? This page maps the components of a build-your-own FinOps data stack. Many teams instead buy a packaged FinOps platform (for example a cloud-native cost tool, or vendors such as Vantage, Cloudability/Apptio, Finout, or nOps) that collapses most of these five layers into one product. Build for control and multi-source unification; buy for speed and lower operational burden. If you are just starting, buying is often the faster path to value.

FOCUS (the FinOps Open Cost and Usage Specification) is an open specification that normalizes billing datasets across AI, cloud, SaaS, data center, and other technology vendors to reduce complexity for FinOps Practitioners. These reference architectures are illustrative decision aids for teams building their own stack; vendor names are examples, not endorsements, and the verdicts are internal-consistency heuristics, not FOCUS conformance certification or a fit assessment for your organization.

The FinOps Open Cost and Usage Specification is published at https://focus.finops.org and is licensed under CC BY 4.0. If a tool here and the specification disagree, the specification is right.
